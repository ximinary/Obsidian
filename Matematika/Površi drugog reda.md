### Elipsoid [[elipsoid.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}+\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1,\quad a,\,b,\,c\geqslant0\end{align}$
(Sfera je ako $a=b=c$)

Parametrizacija:
$\begin{cases}x=a\sin \theta \cos \varphi\\y=b\sin \theta \sin \varphi\\z=c\cos \theta \end{cases},\quad\quad\begin{array}{}\varphi\in[0,\,2\pi]\\\theta\in[0,\,\pi]\end{array}$



### Jednograni hiperboloid [[jednograni hiperboloid.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=1,\quad a,\,b,\,c\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija:
$\begin{cases}x=a\cos \varphi\frac{1}{\cos \theta}\\y=b\sin \varphi\frac{1}{\cos \theta}\\z=c\,\mathrm{tg}\,\theta \end{cases},\quad\quad\begin{array}{}\varphi\in[0,\,2\pi]\\\theta\in(-\frac{\pi}{2},\,\frac{\pi}{2})\end{array}$

### Dvograni hiperboloid [[dvograni hiperboloid.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}-\frac{z^{2}}{c^{2}}=-1,\quad a,\,b,\,c\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija:
$\begin{cases}x=a\cos \varphi\,\mathrm{tg}\,\theta\\y=b\sin \varphi\,\mathrm{tg}\,\theta\\z=\pm c\frac{1}{\cos \theta} \end{cases},\quad\quad\begin{array}{}\varphi\in[0,\,2\pi]\\\theta\in(-\frac{\pi}{2},\,\frac{\pi}{2})\end{array}$

### Konus [[konus.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=z^{2},\quad a,\,b\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija:
$\begin{cases}x=au \cos \varphi\\y=bu\sin \varphi\\z=u\end{cases},\quad\quad\begin{array}{}\varphi\in[0,\,2\pi]\\u\in\mathbb{R}\end{array}$
### Eliptički paraboloid [[elipticki paraboloid.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=z,\quad a,\,b\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija:
$\begin{cases}x=u\\y=v\\z=\frac{u^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{v^{2}}{b^{2}} \end{cases},\quad\quad u,\,v\in\mathbb{R}$
### Hiperbolički paraboloid [[hiperbolicki paraboloid.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=z,\quad a,\,b\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija:
$\begin{cases}x=u\\y=v\\z=\frac{u^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{v^{2}}{b^{2}} \end{cases},\quad\quad u,\,v\in\mathbb{R}$
### Eliptički cilindar [[elipticki cilindar.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1,\quad a,\,b\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija ([[Elipsa|elipse]]):
$\begin{cases}x=a\cos\varphi\\y=b\sin\varphi\\z=u \end{cases},\quad\quad\begin{array}{}\varphi\in[0,\,2\pi]\\u\in\mathbb{R}\end{array}$
### Hiperbolički cilindar [[hiperbolicki cilindar.png|*]]
$\begin{align}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1,\quad a,\,b\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija ([[Hiperbola|hiperbole]]):
$\begin{cases}x=\pm a\frac{1}{\cos\varphi}\\y=b\,\mathrm{tg}\,\varphi\\z=u \end{cases},\quad\quad\begin{array}{}\varphi\in(-\frac{\pi}{2},\,\frac{\pi}{2})\\u\in\mathbb{R}\end{array}$

### Parabolički cilindar [[parabolicki cilindar.png|*]]
$\begin{align}y^{2}=2px,\quad p\geqslant0\end{align}$

Parametrizacija ([[Parabola|parabole]]):
$\begin{cases}x=\frac{u^{2}}{2p}\\y=u\\z=v \end{cases},\quad\quad u,\,v\in\mathbb{R}$
