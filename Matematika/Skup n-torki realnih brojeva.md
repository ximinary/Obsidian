**Def**. Skup svih $n$-[[Teorija skupova#^e21aa8|torki]] realnih brojeva je skup $\mathbb{R}^{n}=\Big\{(x_{1},\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{n})\ \ \Big|\ \ x_{i}\in\mathbb{R},\ \ i=\overline{1,n}\Big\}$

Sabiranje: $\ \:$ $(x_{1},\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{n})+(y_{1},\,y_{2},\,\dots,\,y_{n})=(x_{1}+y_{1},\,x_{2}+y_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{n}+y_{n})$
Množenje skalarom $\lambda\in\mathbb{R}$: $\ \:$ $\lambda\,(x_{1},\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{n})=(\lambda\,x_{1},\,\lambda\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,\lambda\,x_{n})$

Odakle, dobijamo da je $(\mathbb{R}^{n},\,\mathbb{R},\,+,\,\cdot)$ [[Vektorski prostor|vektorski prostor]].

Skalarni proizvod: $\ \:$ $(x_{1},\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{n})\cdot(y_{1},\,y_{2},\,\dots,\,y_{n})=x_{1}\,y_{1}+x_{2}\,y_{2}+\dots+x_{n}\,y_{n}$
uz koji je $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ [[Unitarni prostor. Euklidski prostor|Euklidski prostor]].
[[Unitarni prostor. Euklidski prostor#^ae8620|Norma]]: $\ \:$ $\big|\big|(x_{1},\,x_{2},\,\dots,\,x_{n})\big|\big|=\sqrt[]{x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2}+\dots+x_{n}^{2}}$

### [[Metrika|Metrika]] u $\mathbb{R}^{n}$
**Stav**. Funkcija $\mathrm{d}:\ \mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}\to\mathbb{R}$ $\ \:$ def. sa $\ \:$ $\begin{align}\mathrm{d}(\mathbf{x},\,\mathbf{y})=||\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}||\end{align}$
je metrika u $\mathbb{R}^{n}$
